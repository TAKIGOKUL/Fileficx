Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why do government portals reject photos even when dimensions are correct?

Most portals check three distinct technical factors: pixel dimensions (e.g. 200×230), file size ceiling (e.g. max 50KB), and binary DPI metadata headers (e.g. 200 DPI). If your image has the right pixel dimensions but lacks the standard JFIF resolution density tag, automated portal scripts flag and reject it. Fileficx injects exact DPI headers directly into the JPEG binary stream.

How does Fileficx achieve exact kilobyte targets without making images blurry?

Fileficx uses an adaptive binary search algorithm that optimizes JPEG quantization tables and discrete cosine transform (DCT) coefficients specifically tuned for documents. This maintains sharp contrast on signatures and crisp facial outlines while compressing the file down to the exact requested kilobyte target.

Are my photos, certificates, or signatures stored or logged anywhere?

Never. No files, logs, or personal information leave your device. All processing occurs exclusively in your browser's local RAM and is immediately cleared when you close or refresh the tab.

Can I compress PDF documents to under 300KB for educational portal uploads?

Yes. Fileficx includes an integrated PDF studio capable of stream optimization, page extraction, merging, and image downsampling to meet strict upload ceilings for marksheets, caste certificates, and identity proofs.
